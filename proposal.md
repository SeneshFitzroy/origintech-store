Project Abstract: OriginTech - Smart E-Commerce Platform
Module Leader: Mr. Aruna Kooragamage | Team 01 | March 2026
1. Project Overview
OriginTech is a dual-platform e-commerce solution (responsive web and cross-platform mobile application)
designed for the retail sale of smartphones, tablets, and accessories. The platform addresses three critical market
gaps: product authenticity concerns, the absence of intelligent personalisation, and fragmented customer support
channels. Development follows a two-stage Agile incremental model, with the MVP delivering full commercial
functionality and Stage 2 introducing AI-driven intelligence as a ring-fenced research and development
initiative.

4. Methodology & Differentiation
The project employs a hybrid management framework: predictive lifecycle planning governs well-defined
infrastructure tasks, while Agile sprints (Scrum/Kanban) drive iterative feature delivery. This approach, aligned
with PMI value-driven principles, enables the team to respond to evolving requirements without compromising
delivery milestones. OriginTech is differentiated through its built-in authenticity verification, a
sustainability-focused trade-in programme, and a clearly defined AI personalisation roadmap that positions the
platform as a trusted retail partner rather than a conventional marketplace.
